I'm currently a 4th year undergraduate at the University of Idaho, graduating in Spring 2027. My undergraduate research experience has included numerical relativity and particle geophysics, both primarily as modeling and analysis. I've contributed to the [NRPy](https://github.com/nrpy/nrpy) numerical relativity framework and built a [standalone visualization pipeline](https://github.com/RalsG/bh_vis) for creating 3D animations of binary black hole mergers (currently compatible with BH@H, and ET data formats, as well as the SXS and RIT merger databases).
