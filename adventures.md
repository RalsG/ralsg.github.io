Views while attempting a summit of Granite Peak - Montana's tallest point. We ended up turning around about halfway up the final face after weather moved in and we were unable to find the expected rappel rings. We later learned that there were no rappel rings because we'd veered off our intended route (oops). It was the right call and a fall would've absolutely been fatal, but I'm coming back for this one at some point.
